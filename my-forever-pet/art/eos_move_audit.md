# Explorers of Sky move audit
Reference data: damage-eos (MIT; see DAMAGE_EOS_LICENSE.txt), mechanics.cpp MOVES[559]. Numeric fields and enum identifiers only, with no sprites or audio. 559 entries include unused/internal IDs; they are not 559 playable moves. Original EoS has no Fairy type; Kindria retains its own Fairy moves. Reference range and animation IDs are manually cross-checked against https://mysterydungeonwiki.com/wiki/Explorers_of_Sky:List_of_Moves and https://wiki.skytemple.org/List_of_Effect_Animations. Socket/trajectory labels are implementation interpretations, not frame-verified animation geometry. ThunderShock effect ID remains unresolved. No full original effect table or ROM assets are packaged.
All 559 records have power, PP, two accuracy values, hits, crit, type and category; unverified range and animation entries are explicitly null. Nine original Kindria moves now use EoS reference data. Original naming and visuals retained. The actual damage formula remains Kindria's bounded ratio; precise EoS formula/AI, statuses, ally targeting, diagonal aiming, weather, accuracy stages and per-move art review remain pending. Earthquake presently only damages enemies because allied combat is not implemented. No claim of commercial legal clearance.
