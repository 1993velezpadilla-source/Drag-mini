# Full EoS roster integration, iteration 1
468 identified moves, 233 direct damage prototypes, 235 awaiting special rules or range confirmation. All original numerical fields and effect candidates retained in eos_move_audit.json. Added IDs eos_000..eos_558 are reserved in the save format; existing 54 slots unchanged. Prototype unlock levels are Kindria-original, not universal EoS learn levels (which differ by species). Damage still uses Kindria's model, not exact EoS. Accuracy uses current two-stage model. Single-hit basic damage is active, secondary effects, target-specific visual choreography, status and multi-hit moves remain incomplete. Shared original effect families are placeholders pending visual review, not final art. No proprietary assets imported. 

## Iteration 2: multi-hit prototype
16 additional moves, 249 active EoS-derived prototypes, 219 pending rules; 303 total Kindria moves. One PP and one enemy turn per burst, separate hit attempts, stop on target defeat. Fixed 2 or provisional uniform 2–5 hits; exact distribution, critical application, projectile retargeting, secondary effects and individual VFX remain pending. Existing IDs retained. Internal slots 543+ with mismatched embedded IDs are reported separately rather than treated as verified move rows; ordinary slot mismatches still fail. Tests added; Godot and CI validation pending. ## Iteration 3: HP flow
Two new self-healing moves (Recover and Slack Off references), plus five existing damage prototypes upgraded to drain health. 251 EoS prototypes, 217 pending, 305 total. Self-heal spends one PP and turn when injured, 50% max HP with cap; full-HP cast rejected as a Kindria UX rule. Drain uses 50% of actual HP removed, floor with minimum 1 for positive damage; overkill/rounding are provisional Kindria decisions. Energy return path and self-centered healing particles added using existing original sprite families. Heal Order deferred due conflicting reference values.
