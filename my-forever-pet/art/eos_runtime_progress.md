# Full EoS roster integration, iteration 1
468 identified moves, 233 direct damage prototypes, 235 awaiting special rules or range confirmation. All original numerical fields and effect candidates retained in eos_move_audit.json. Added IDs eos_000..eos_558 are reserved in the save format; existing 54 slots unchanged. Prototype unlock levels are Kindria-original, not universal EoS learn levels (which differ by species). Damage still uses Kindria's model, not exact EoS. Accuracy uses current two-stage model. Single-hit basic damage is active, secondary effects, target-specific visual choreography, status and multi-hit moves remain incomplete. Shared original effect families are placeholders pending visual review, not final art. No proprietary assets imported.

## Iteration 3: HP flow
Two new self-healing moves (Recover and Slack Off references), plus five existing damage prototypes upgraded to drain health. 251 EoS prototypes, 217 pending, 305 total. ## Iteration 6: enemy status core

Enemy-side sleep 3–6, yawn 3, confusion 7–12, paralysis 2–3, freeze 1–3, poison/burn/toxic persistent with 2/6/6 HP ticks at 20/20/2 turn intervals; burn reduces damage, major statuses replace each other, sleep separate. Current limitations: sleep wake-on-hit not implemented; confusion random direction but no friendly fire, simplified confused attacks; paralysis simplified movement; enemy status only, not full symmetrical player/party engine; exact secondary chance provisional 10%; type immunity, bosses, timing and art require further review. ## Iteration 8: movement speed
8 new moves (7 speed moves and Haze), Skull Bash charge upgraded. 311 EoS prototypes / 365 total / 157 pending. Player travel boosts stack independently to 3, last 4–5 full turns, allow extra movement; a successful speed boost grants an immediate extra action. Enemies slowed act every second turn over 3–4 afflicted turns. Haze resets stages in current room without clearing travel speed. Skull Bash grants temporary defense during charge. Kindria scheduler is simplified: slowdown does not yet refresh player actions, enemy positive speed, party-targeted effects, secondary speed changes and exact original turn ordering pending. No Defense Curl/Rollout multiplier added (not applicable in EoS). Original visual families remain temporary. ## Iteration 9: shields and retaliation
11 new moves, 322 EoS prototypes / 376 total / 146 pending. One mutually exclusive shield group, source durations: Protect/Detect 3–6, Endure 4–5, Reflect/Light Screen 11–12, Counter/Pursuit/Payback 7–12, Mirror Coat 11–12, Metal Burst 8–11, Safeguard 16–20. Screens halve matching damage, Endure clamps to 1 HP, Protect blocks enemy attacks and move effects, Safeguard blocks current poison/weaken/mark effects. Adjacent counters queue actual HP damage and resolve after enemy phase to avoid invalidating iteration; Metal Burst targets all adjacent enemies at half damage. Provisional differences: counter timing, no retaliation after fatal blow, no projectile/explosion system, one companion, current enemy damage categories, no individual VFX. Substitute, Mist, Magic Coat and Lucky Chant deferred until supporting systems exist. Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Status_condition_(Mystery_Dungeon) . CI pending.
