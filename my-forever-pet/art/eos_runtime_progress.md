# Full EoS roster integration, iteration 1
468 identified moves, 233 direct damage prototypes, 235 awaiting special rules or range confirmation. All original numerical fields and effect candidates retained in eos_move_audit.json. Added IDs eos_000..eos_558 are reserved in the save format; existing 54 slots unchanged. Prototype unlock levels are Kindria-original, not universal EoS learn levels (which differ by species). Damage still uses Kindria's model, not exact EoS. Accuracy uses current two-stage model. Single-hit basic damage is active, secondary effects, target-specific visual choreography, status and multi-hit moves remain incomplete. Shared original effect families are placeholders pending visual review, not final art. No proprietary assets imported. 

## Iteration 2: multi-hit prototype
16 additional moves, 249 active EoS-derived prototypes, 219 pending rules; 303 total Kindria moves. One PP and one enemy turn per burst, separate hit attempts, stop on target defeat. Fixed 2 or provisional uniform 2–5 hits; exact distribution, critical application, projectile retargeting, secondary effects and individual VFX remain pending. Existing IDs retained. Internal slots 543+ with mismatched embedded IDs are reported separately rather than treated as verified move rows; ordinary slot mismatches still fail. Tests added; Godot and CI validation pending. ## Iteration 3: HP flow
Two new self-healing moves (Recover and Slack Off references), plus five existing damage prototypes upgraded to drain health. 251 EoS prototypes, 217 pending, 305 total. Self-heal spends one PP and turn when injured, 50% max HP with cap; full-HP cast rejected as a Kindria UX rule. Drain uses 50% of actual HP removed, floor with minimum 1 for positive damage; overkill/rounding are provisional Kindria decisions. Energy return path and self-centered healing particles added using existing original sprite families. Heal Order deferred due conflicting reference values. Reference: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ds/955859-pokemon-mystery-dungeon-explorers-of-sky/faqs/55728 ; https://mysterydungeonwiki.com/wiki/Explorers_of_Sky:Giga_Drain_TM . CI pending.

## Iteration 4: escalating hits and recoil
3 new moves (Ice Ball, Rollout and Triple Kick references) and 7 previously active moves upgraded to max-HP recoil. 254 active EoS prototypes, 214 pending, 308 total. Rollout/Ice Ball use 1.0–1.4 damage scaling in .1 increments; Triple Kick 1/2/3; stop on miss or target KO. Recoil uses 1/8 max HP for Submission, Double-Edge, Take Down, Volt Tackle, Wood Hammer, Brave Bird, 1/4 for Head Smash. Fatal recoil ends expedition without an extra enemy attack; no recoil on miss. Kindria rounding and current damage formula retained, no abilities/Defense Curl integration yet. ## Iteration 5: charge and release
Eight existing moves upgraded, count remains 254 EoS prototypes / 308 total. Charge consumes PP once; next attempted turn releases in original direction even with zero PP or no remaining target. One enemy turn during charge and one on release. Dig/Fly/Bounce/Shadow Force use provisional semi-invulnerability against current enemy attack roster, Shadow Force ignores current guard. Focus Punch remains charged even after damage. Double final damage under current Kindria damage model. Distinct underground, airborne, vanish and gather presentation states. Dive deferred pending water tile rules; Skull Bash deferred pending temporary defense stages. Specific counter-moves, weather, IQ, exact art and final formula pending.
