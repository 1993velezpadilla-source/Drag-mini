# My Forever Pet gold asset gauntlet

Adapted from the actual City Zero specialist vehicle gate and the Porsche five-view Blender/Godot workflow. Original models, one hero candidate at a time, no substitution of concept images for real rendered frames.

1. Editable master BLEND and exact GLB SHA256, named parts, PBR materials and measured world dimensions.
2. Distinct mobile LOD with explicit triangle cap, parts and dimensions retained; compare silhouette masks from five calibrated native Blender Cycles views. This compares master to mobile only, NOT concept art accuracy.
3. Independent Godot import, actual five engine captures, articulated foot/pad verification. No claim of real-device performance from Mesa.
4. Explicit visual review (all eight categories >=4/5, tied to mobile hash), then physical Android evidence (p95 <=33.3 ms, device and exact asset hash). Automated review NEVER creates approvals.
5. Candidate is outside production assets. Current gameplay lab is unchanged. A future promotion must run the release gate with --require-release. Missing evidence rejects promotion. No candidate is declared AAA or shipped just because a workflow passes.

First candidate: Mosskit, original source-authored parametric sculpt with separate feet, paws, facial parts, ears and tail. New mesh retains the green pet identity. Review before proceeding to bridge, arches, castle islands, props and moving hazards.
